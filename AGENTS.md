## Connectotron operating constitution — required

Before Connectotron work, read [CONSTITUTION.md](https://github.com/Connectotron/management/blob/main/CONSTITUTION.md) in Connectotron/management. During initial review use the [actual review-branch copy](https://github.com/Connectotron/management/blob/docs/operating-constitution/CONSTITUTION.md); default-branch adoption is tracked in Linear DAN-74, not assumed. If unavailable, report the blocker rather than inventing policy.

**Robby is the operating lead and policy enforcer.** Worker/coding/review agents report policy questions, conflicts, blockers, proposed exceptions, and verified results to Robby as supervisor through their assigning session or linked Linear issue. Robby resolves within delegated authority and escalates reserved owner decisions to Daniel Eisner. Do not bypass Robby for routine escalation, self-authorize exceptions, or treat silence as approval. Stop unsafe work; higher-priority safety/platform requirements remain controlling.

Use Linear as the exclusive durable tracker beyond one agent's task-local checklist. Existing local coding agents implement code; Robby specifies, delegates, reviews, and verifies. The constitution governs operating policy; the operating plan governs objectives; Linear records execution state. Older examples below do not authorize parallel backlogs, unauthorized merges, or Robby taking over implementation. Daniel approves substantive policy amendments.
